Marginalia: a notebook that listens.

A notebook for raw thoughts. Apple's on-device language model reads what you write and offers tasks, calendar events and reminders while you type. There's no backend, and your notes never leave the phone.

One line in, a plan out

You write the way you think: "Call mom tomorrow at six about Sunday, and buy a lamp for the hallway before the weekend." Marginalia suggests an event (Call mom, Tue 18:00) and a task (Buy a lamp, due Friday). You confirm with one tap.
