Do customers come back?

About 20,000 consumer orders across two brands, one row per product. The questions: how long until a customer buys again, what they buy next, and who has quietly stopped. About 85% buy only once, and those who come back take around eight months. I built the analysis as reusable SQL views in SQLite, split every result by brand and sales channel, and wrote up the findings for the business. Most of the work was deciding which rows to trust.

Illustrative figures throughout: the method is real, the numbers are changed and rounded.
