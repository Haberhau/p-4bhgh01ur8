The Spanish fashion market bifurcated.

Master's thesis, EAE Business School Barcelona, 2026. With Ahra Ko and Elias Saifildin.

Inditex's FY2025 annual report doesn't name Shein or Temu as competitive threats. Five independent models, built on scraped retail prices and Eurostat trade data, say it should. Our response for the incumbent is Wardrobe-as-a-Service: a €35 a month rotation of three Zara pieces, built on the one asset the disruptors can't copy, clothes worth repairing.
