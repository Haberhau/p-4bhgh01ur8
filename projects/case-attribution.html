How many click & collect frames get our lenses?

At a premium eyewear manufacturer, customers reserve up to three frames online and try them on at a local optician. Nobody tracked whether the frame they kept was then glazed with the company's own lenses. I linked CRM appointments, order items, lab orders and actual net sales into one attribution model, then measured how much of the answer was noise.

Illustrative figures throughout: the method is real, the numbers are changed and rounded.

What the numbers said

Matching on the optician alone finds a lens order for about half of all click & collect orders. That looks like a result until you check the baseline: the same opticians order lenses in two out of three random windows. Only matching on the frame carries signal, and it lands at about 2% of kept frames.

Match
Account, frame, −1 to +60 days
Denominator

Outcome
