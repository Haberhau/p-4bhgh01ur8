How many click & collect frames get our lenses?

At Silhouette, customers can reserve up to three frames online and try them on at a local optician. Nobody tracked whether the frame they kept was then glazed with our own lenses. The answer sat in three systems that were never built to talk to each other. I linked CRM appointments, order items, lab orders and actual net sales into one attribution model, and built in checks for how much of any match was just noise.

Company-internal project: this page shows how I worked, not the results.
