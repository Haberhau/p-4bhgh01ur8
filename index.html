My journey

I played volleyball from age ten until a knee injury ended my career. I chose business school in Kufstein over taking on our family dealership.

As a Digital Business Intern at Silhouette, I built dashboards and forecasting models, established the CRM data structure and helped test Salesforce. As Data Analyst, I owned B2C reporting, led the business side of the Salesforce rollouts and became technical system owner.

In Barcelona I continued as a Data Analyst for 25 hours a week. One of my assignments was to act as Scrum Master while we brought B2C fulfilment in-house. In the evenings I studied Big Data & Analytics at EAE.

At my desk I build apps and plugins with AI and practise independent analysis on public data. Are you ready to be part of my journey?
